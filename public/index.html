AI assistant

  
Optional. Works with any AI provider: Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI, Google Gemini, Mistral, Groq, OpenRouter, DeepSeek, xAI, Together, a local Ollama or LM Studio, or any other OpenAI-compatible API (enter its base URL). When you click "Ask AI", an excerpt of the sample (first 6 KB) and AutoChef's best attempts are sent to the provider you chose. Do not use a cloud provider for samples you may not share; a local model keeps everything on your machine. The key is kept in the AutoChef process memory only and is forgotten when it stops.

  

    
    
  

  
  

    
    
    
  

  

  

  
Threat-intel lookups

  
Optional and off until you add a key. Nothing is sent until you click Look up next to a finding, and then only that one value goes to the services below. Bitcoin addresses are checked on Blockstream without a key; other wallets get explorer links. Keys are kept in memory only.
